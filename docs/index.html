Why it's different

🫧

Always there, never in the way

Docks to a screen edge and stays on top of every app, on every virtual desktop — a tap away, out of your way otherwise.

🔒

100% local

No account, no server, no telemetry. Your todos are stored on your machine and never leave it.

⚡

Instant open/close

One click expands it into a full list right where the bubble sits. Click away and it snaps back to just the bubble.

🧲

Remembers where you left it

Position, expanded state, and every todo persist across restarts — pick up exactly where you left off.

🖱️

Drag to reorder

Reorder, complete, and delete todos with simple drag-and-drop and one-click interactions.

🖥️

Tray icon fallback

Show or hide the bubble, launch at login, or quit — all from a lightweight system tray menu.
